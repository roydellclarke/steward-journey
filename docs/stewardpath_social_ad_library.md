# StewardPath — Social Ad Library (trigger-led, loss-averse, JTBD-anchored)

## Consistency guardrails (every ad obeys these)
- **Name the loss, never promise a price.** The loss you avert is being *unprepared and exposed* — rushed, outmatched, the wrong successor, your people and name unprotected. Never imply "we'll get you more money" — that's broker/valuation territory you've stayed out of.
- **Calm, low-pressure CTA.** Private, free to start, no obligation. No countdown-timer urgency — pressure repels a 55+ owner and breaks the brand.
- **Stay in lane.** Preparation, not advice. Not a broker. Nothing shared without the owner's say-so.
- **Beachhead first.** Lead with trade/HVAC-specific language; general founder-led variants are for later expansion.

## Channel-fit reality
- **Facebook (primary for owners):** 55+ trade owners live here. Conversational, image-led.
- **LinkedIn:** best for the *advisor* channel and larger/professional-services owners. More composed tone.
- **YouTube:** catches high-intent moments ("I got an offer"). Short spoken pre-roll.
- **Instagram:** secondary for this demographic; reuse Facebook copy, trim shorter.

## Reusable CTA / disclaimer block (append to ads)

## Targeting hints (aim at the beachhead)
- **Facebook/Instagram:** age 50+, owners/self-employed, interests in HVAC/plumbing/electrical/contracting trade associations, trade brands and suppliers, small-business-owner interest clusters; geo = national.
- **LinkedIn (advisors):** job titles — CPA, exit planner, financial advisor, estate attorney, commercial banker. **LinkedIn (owners):** titles "Owner/President/Founder," company size 11–200, industries = construction/HVAC/facilities/manufacturing.
- **YouTube:** keyword/intent targeting on "sell my business," "business broker," "unsolicited offer for my business," and trade-association content.

## A/B test guidance
Test the **trigger hook** first (which fear/job pulls hardest), then the CTA. Keep the disclaimer block constant. Watch cost-per-qualified-lead, not clicks — and remember every number here is a hypothesis until Phase 0 confirms the language owners actually use.
