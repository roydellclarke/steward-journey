# StewardPath — Master Strategy (v1)

> The single current-state strategy, consolidating all workstreams. Everything marked *(hypothesis)* is pending Phase 0 validation — expect this document to be revised by what real owners say and do. Companion documents are indexed at the end.

## 1. Thesis
A demographically certain wave of founder-led business owners must transition ownership over the next 10–15 years; ~70–80% are unprepared; and no one owns the **upstream, trust-first, unregulated preparation layer** that sits before brokers, valuations, and advisors. StewardPath owns it: *"Before you sell, decide what must be protected."*

## 2. Positioning
- **Protection-first, not transaction-first.** The category is "transition readiness," not exit-planning software. We monetize preparation and deliberately never touch the regulated transaction (no brokerage, valuation, legal, tax, or investment advice — regulated work routes to humans).
- **Upstream of, and partnered with, the ecosystem.** Brokers and advisors are distribution and referral partners, not competitors — we feed them prepared, qualified owners.
- **Trust and confidentiality ARE the product.** Visible security, default-private, owner-controlled disclosure. The brand never pressures.
- **The concierge is engineered, not hired.** AI delivers 4 of the 5 concierge jobs (interpretation, sequencing, accountability, reassurance); one thin, fixed-time human touchpoint supplies the 5th (authority). ~85–90% gross margins vs ~30–40% for human consultants.
- **Accompaniment, not artifact.** A longitudinal program (intake → roadmap → re-scoring) rather than a one-time report.

## 3. Beachhead (DE Steps 1–2) *(hypothesis)*
**Founder-led HVAC / plumbing / trades shops, ~15–75 employees, owner 55+, getting or fearing unsolicited roll-up offers. National, online-led.**
- Why: only segment scoring high on every DE criterion — ability to pay, tight national trade communities (word-of-mouth), a *live* compelling trigger (the PE roll-up wave means the unsolicited-offer moment is firing segment-wide), perfect product fit (founder-dependent, undocumented), and clean expansion to adjacent trades.
- National discipline: homogeneity comes from **vertical + trigger**, not geography. Own "trade owners in the roll-up crosshairs" before broadening.
- Expansion path: adjacent trades → local professional services → family specialty manufacturers → general founder-led.

## 4. Language strategy (two registers, one product)
- **Owner-facing:** trade talk, never buyer-speak — "the shop," "your crew," "the name on your truck," "can it run without you when you're off the truck," "the maintenance base," "the roll-up guys keep calling." (Full bank in the language doc.)
- **Advisor/machine-facing** (LinkedIn, llms.txt, advisor materials): the polished register — "succession-readiness program."
- All copy is loss-averse: the loss averted is being *unprepared and exposed* (rushed, outmatched, wrong successor, crew and name unprotected) — never "get a higher price."
- *(hypothesis)* All language to be replaced with verbatim owner phrasing from Phase 0 / past-seller interviews. Language-market fit comes from their mouths, not our drafts.

## 5. Business model & pricing posture
Three revenue lines, staged:
1. **Owner readiness** *(core, unregulated)* — free sample → self-serve report (~$249–499) → AI-delivered concierge (~$1,500–3,000+, test above $3k). No cheap paid tier: low prices destroy trust in a life-stakes decision; the free sample is the only low rung. Compare against advisory fees (5-figure+), not software.
2. **Advisor subscription** *(distribution + recurring)* — ~$199/mo pilot, laddering toward ~$999 with proof. Borrows advisor trust; the answer to CAC.
3. **Qualified warm-handoff / deal-flow** *(upside, later)* — prepared, readiness-scored owners are premium lower-middle-market leads; structure carefully vs referral-fee regulation.
Pricing tiers must be cleanly separated (software → software+light touch → human-led) and size-tiered by revenue/employee band. **All prices are hypotheses until Phase 0 commitments confirm them** — keep exact numbers out of cached AI-facing files until then.

## 6. Product strategy (built; brief = v3)
- Adaptive, form-forward intake (not a chatbot) with deterministic branching, reflective-summary moments ("the system heard me"), graceful "I don't know" handling, save/resume, and emotional sequencing.
- Structured intake-state (`Field<T>` wrapper) as source of truth; **no RAG for session recall** — RAG reserved for benchmarking, knowledge base, and large documents.
- Explainable readiness score (per-driver "why"), grounded synthesis (no fabrication), successor-fit and advisor-ready outputs.
- Visible confidentiality UX at every sensitive ask; banded data (privacy + future benchmarking moat).
- Auth: anonymous start → passwordless email code/magic-link at the save and report gates. No social login.
- Human touchpoint: one scheduled review fed by an AI-prepped handoff package; fixed time per customer.

## 7. Go-to-market & acquisition
- **Triggers are the targeting.** Lead trigger: the unsolicited offer (highest intent + loss aversion). Others: peer sold, health scare, burnout, kids don't want it, key person left, wealth locked up.
- **Channels by honesty, not fashion:** Facebook = primary for 55+ trade owners; LinkedIn = advisors and larger owners; YouTube = high-intent offer-moment searches; trade associations, peer groups (Nexstar, Service Roundtable, etc.), trade media.
- **AI-era discoverability (GEO):** own the trigger-questions in AI answers and search. Priority order: (1) genuinely authoritative answer pages in owner language, (2) SEO + schema, (3) robots.txt allowing AI crawlers (each named group must repeat private disallows), (4) markdown mirror of content pages, (5) llms.txt (emerging convention — cheap hygiene, not a backbone). Content routing is worthless until the answer pages exist — write them in validated owner language.
- **Advisor channel is the COCA answer** for a trust-gated, once-in-a-lifetime purchase.

## 8. Validation discipline (the gate everything waits behind)
- **Phase 0 (now, no build):** 5–10 design-partner conversations in the beachhead — founder-led trade owners 55+, ideally offer-recipients — plus past-sellers (found via consolidator acquisition announcements, trade brokers, peer groups, LinkedIn "former owner" searches). Past-sellers validate the *job* and hand over the language; current owners validate *willingness to pay*.
- **The gate: H1 (will they share) + H3 (real money down).** Verbal yes = no. Deposits, pre-orders, signed commitments only.
- **Decision rule:** share+pay → build Phase 1 MVP. Share+value but won't pay → pivot advisor-first. Won't share → stop and rethink; the trust premise failed.
- Phases: 0 by-hand → 1 self-serve MVP (rules-only adaptivity) → 2 program (re-scoring, LLM clarifiers, productized touchpoint) → 3 moat & channel (benchmarking RAG, advisor scale, deal-flow).

## 10. Moat (compounding order)
Trust brand → accumulated readiness data → peer benchmarking ("owners like you") → category ownership → prepared-deal-flow network. The data moat only exists because the schema was banded and aggregation-ready from day one.

## 11. Top risks & mitigations
- **Trust/adoption** → confidentiality-first UX + advisor channel.
- **Willingness to pay** → Phase 0 real-commitment gate before any scale spend.
- **COCA breaking LTV:COCA (>3:1 needed)** → advisor channel + trigger-content engine; model per channel early.
- **Over-building before validation** → the phase gates; the MVP defer list; "capture data now, productize later."
- **Regulatory line** → stay strictly in preparation; route regulated work out; structure referral fees carefully.
- **Language misfire** → two registers; replace all copy with verbatim owner language post-Phase 0.
