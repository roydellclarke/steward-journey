# StewardPath, one-page overview

*The handoff on your terms.*

## What it is

StewardPath is a private, guided program for people who built a business and now face selling it, passing it on, or stepping away. It walks the owner through the decision before a broker or buyer frames it for them. The owner answers questions at their own pace, sees where they stand, and leaves with a plan and a real person to call.

It prepares the owner. It does not give legal, tax, or valuation advice, and it sends that work to qualified humans.

## Who it serves

Owners of founder-led and family businesses, often aged 55 and up, frequently with no clear successor and a deep stake in their people, customers, and name. Many are not comfortable with software. The whole experience is built for them: plain words, a calm pace, and a person who stays with them.

## What the owner gets

- **A guided check that listens.** Easy questions first. The hard ones come later, with a word of reassurance before each. "I don't know" is a fine answer.
- **A readiness score they can read.** One number across five plain areas: the books, how well the business runs without them, what is written down, whether the family agrees, and how ready they feel to let go. Each score shows the reason behind it.
- **A clear next step, not just a report.** The owner names what they refuse to lose, weighs who could carry the business forward, and rules out the paths they would never accept.
- **Briefs they can hand over.** A summary for an advisor, a guide for the family, a profile of the right successor. Every word comes from what the owner told us, nothing invented.
- **A plan they return to.** The work is saved. The owner comes back, and the score climbs as they prepare.

## Why people trust it

Privacy is the product, not a footnote. Everything stays private by default. The owner chooses what is ever shared, and with whom. Nothing is used to train AI. They can export or delete their data at any time. When they want a person, a private review is one click away.
